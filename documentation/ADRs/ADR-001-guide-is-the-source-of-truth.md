## ADR 001: The Guide is the source of truth; the reference projects are evidence

### Status
Accepted

### Context

Three Spring Boot projects in this workspace share one design through a lineage: BugTracker, then wpmanager,
then `backend/`. The same defects travelled down that lineage, and 34 of wpmanager's 88 findings are shared
with `backend/`. Consistency across a lineage is not evidence of correctness; it is evidence of inheritance.
The projects are rated about 6.5/10 for design and 3.5/10 for implementation, so neither can be copied as
the convention. At the same time the 241 findings are the only evidence base we have, and a rule with no
evidence is an invention. On 2026-10-03 the brief was changed from "grounded, not invented" to "grounded or
corrected, always cited" to hold both at once. Decision D1 of
[[Features/to-do/Spring-Boot-Architecture-Guide-and-Base-Project]] records the outcome; review decision F14
moved the traceability-matrix scope into [[ADRs/ADR-016-guide-document-format-and-rule-ids|ADR-016]].

**Evidence:**
[[Docs/backend/Reviews/01-Security-Review#BE-R01-01|BE-R01-01]],
[[Docs/BugTracker/Reviews/01-Security-and-Multi-Tenancy-Review#BT-R01-01|BT-R01-01]] and
[[Docs/wpmanager/Reviews/01-Security-Review#WP-R01-01|WP-R01-01]] — no enforced authorization in any of the
three;
[[Docs/backend/Reviews/02-CRUD-Framework-and-API-Design-Review#BE-R02-01|BE-R02-01]],
[[Docs/BugTracker/Reviews/02-CRUD-Framework-and-API-Design-Review#BT-R02-01|BT-R02-01]] and
[[Docs/wpmanager/Reviews/02-CRUD-Framework-and-API-Design-Review#WP-R02-01|WP-R02-01]] — the same no-op base
`update` carried through the lineage;
[[Docs/backend/Reviews/03-Domain-Model-and-Persistence-Review#BE-R03-04|BE-R03-04]],
[[Docs/BugTracker/Reviews/03-Domain-Model-and-Persistence-Review#BT-R03-09|BT-R03-09]] and
[[Docs/wpmanager/Reviews/06-Domain-Model-and-Persistence-Review#WP-R06-02|WP-R06-02]] — schema generated by
the ORM instead of migrations, in all three.

### Decision

1. We will state every rule of the convention once, in the Guide under `documentation/Docs/Guide/`. Nothing
   else in the workspace restates a rule.
2. We will treat the three reference projects and their 241 findings as evidence, not authority. A finding
   supports or challenges a rule; it never dictates one.
3. We will have every Guide rule cite at least one Finding ID or one ADR, so a reader can check the claim.
4. We will make every departure from the reference projects explicit in the Guide, in a "Differs From the
   Reference Projects" field, so a reader who knows those projects is never surprised.
5. We will have the Base Project and the Skill cite Rule IDs and never restate rules, so a rule has exactly
   one place to change.

**Alternatives rejected:**
- Copy the newest project (`backend/`) — it inherits the lineage's defects and its implementation is rated
  the lowest of the three.
- Take the consensus of the three — a lineage copies its defects, so agreement proves inheritance, not
  correctness.
- Design from nothing — loses the familiar shape the user story 3 asks for and discards 241 findings of
  evidence.

### Consequences

- A rule has one place to change, and the Skill and the Base Project follow automatically.
- Rules can be challenged with evidence, because every one of them names the finding or the decision it
  rests on.
- The Guide becomes a maintained artifact: the Guide, the Base Project and the Skill can drift, and the
  validator plus the traceability matrix exist to detect it.
- Finding IDs must stay stable forever, because accepted ADRs and Guide rules cite them by ID.
- The memory-bank rule "a pattern that only appears in one project needs stronger justification" is
  replaced: a pattern does not have to appear in all three projects, but every rule cites its evidence.
